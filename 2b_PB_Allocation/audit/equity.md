# Ability to Pay and geography

## Does the national allocation follow Hjalsted?

### Takeaway

Yes for Eq. 5 on the covered countries. No for calling the producer-GVA upscaling Hjalsted’s published product method. Table 2, Eq. 6 uses the local share of final consumption and Eq. 7 adds a green incentive. Sections 2.5.1 and 2.6 identify consumers in the case studies. Its 2017 alpha is 0.243, explicitly printed in the Table 2 footnote. [Original published paper at DTU](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/234548766/Hjalsted_2020_.pdf)

### Cited Findings

- Write population P_c, income per capita y_c=G_c/P_c, global totals P and G and global mean y=G/P. Individual PB share is B/P × (y/y_c) × alpha. Summing across individuals and imposing total B gives alpha=P²/[G sum(P_c²/G_c)] and national weight w_c=(P_c²/G_c)/sum(P_i²/G_i). Weights are dimensionless, independent of B, and unaffected by a uniform currency-unit conversion. An average of persons’ inverse incomes within a national population is a different rule. Computing country weights before regional aggregation preserves the defined allocation. Collapsing countries first suppresses the sum of inverse-income weights by convexity. This tends to inflate wealthy singleton country shares after renormalisation, but there is no uniform sign for all sectors. ### Inferences

The hybrid combines a legitimate ethical country-level prioritarian rule with attribution to production income in an explicitly chosen region. The relevant stakeholders shift from consumers/patients to producing regions. GDP/head represents average national capacity, not the company’s resources, distribution among patients, or distribution of earnings within a country. If profits go to overseas owners, procurement in a poorer economy does not demonstrate a benefit to vulnerable residents. Product intent (healthcare) gives no measurable need-based priority under either GDP method. To make a faithful Hjalsted comparison, identify the consumers/payers and local final-consumption category and spend shares, with the service/product boundary stated. A GVA-upscaled variant should be called “producer-region ATP × total GVA”.

### Gaps

The paper’s original 2017 national observations and country handling were not independently reconciled economy by economy. Current archived values were recalculated, but no claim of byte-for-byte equality to the authors’ vintage is warranted. IMF snapshots are trimmed; source archive suffices for these input rows.

### Inferences

The dataset geographic mix measures the 2022 average UK chemical industry. No traceable country-specific manufacturing/first-tier procurement roster for this hypothetical mAb product was supplied. Facility, depreciation, electricity, QC, treatment services and UK bought-in inputs can all embody imported capital or supplies; labour generally has a stronger domestic basis. A scope that excludes embodied capital elsewhere should say so explicitly. Supplier headquarters, distributor, direct manufacturing site, upstream substrate origin and patients must be distinguished. The mixed rest-of-world groups also contain some wealthy countries; the script’s high-income filter excludes all mixed groups. Procurement choices need category-specific UK/foreign spend shares (with uncertainty), actual points of manufacture and an upstream partition on a consistent value basis. Geographic variants should also vary environmental supply mix and transport where physical choices differ. Only denominator variation is appropriate for a pure ethical scenario with unchanged physical design. Holding LCA fixed while attributing sourcing differences cannot quantify environmental impact of genuinely relocating procurement.

### Gaps

Raw SuperPro cost rows used in geographical scenarios are covered by the economic audit. These are model expense categories, not measured transactional origins. The authors' version-specific country partition should be obtained to certify the proposed crosswalk; the alternative above uses a named industrial-ecology concordance and establishes that the project’s macroregion shortcut is material.

## What use in the MILP is justified?

### Takeaway

Conditional ethical scenario suitable for a labelled frozen-budget sensitivity, once the linear environmental model is independently validated. Numerical replication does not validate a binding product entitlement. At present, rank alternative designs as exploratory; scientific claims about absolute performance or fairness need an agreed unit of ethical responsibility, mutually compatible boundary and geographic scope, and representative environmental coverage.

### Cited Findings

- Hjalsted’s conservation argument guarantees that person/country shares sum to the global SOS. Multiplying by whole-chain GVA weights across all gross-output sectors does not preserve that property: the same value added can be embodied in an intermediate producer’s sales and again downstream. A unique final-demand decomposition with embodied GVA, or mutually exclusive direct-VA accounts, would allow an exhaustive partition when economic and environmental system boundaries are aligned. Direct-only allocation would require clearly defining which environmental burdens the actor is assuming. [Hjalsted](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/234548766/Hjalsted_2020_.pdf) [Hartmann](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13333010/)
- For a fixed common service demand, exogenous cost and geographic weights, budgets are constants and linear constraints are straightforward. If siting or purchasing affects those weights, re-estimating the budget per design rewards moving nominal spend into regions with very high SOS/EUR. Higher spend at fixed service, or more batches than needed, can relax a budget. Determine explicitly whether the optimizer should allocate responsibility ex ante with fixed ethically justified weights, or model reallocation, in which case matched physical changes, prevention of double claims, and an external total cap become necessary. [Regional budget implementation](https://github.com/ALIFEPLAYS/FYR_code/blob/035538e33cd0223a5805ece2632e86b03e35eac4/2b_PB_Allocation/pb_allocation.py)

### Inferences

Require a defined responsible population/market, matched geography with full-chain versus first-tier rationale, and actual foreground sourcing or credible stratified ranges before calling this the network's actual budget. Retain separate accounting of measured, modelled, and imputed inputs. For a paper, 153.5% supports a conditional exceedance of the characterised subtotal under the chosen model, with direction of remaining inventory conditional on additions/credits and on possible corrections to current loads. It cannot certify the network's absolute incompatibility with planetary boundaries.

### Gaps

No empirical supplier-origin distribution, country-of-use distribution or joint probability law for costs, geography and impact factors supports probabilistic confidence intervals. The envelope’s scope should be reported accordingly.
