# Auditing planetary boundary budgets for mAb production

**Total GVA is defensible as the primary allocation for an exploratory MILP. Producer-region Ability-to-Pay is defensible as an explicitly normative alternative. Neither currently supports a binding claim about the network’s absolute environmental sustainability.** Independent calculations reproduce their respective central climate utilisations of **64.90% and 153.46%**. Both divide a partially characterised footprint by a budget derived from the same unobserved bulk-product value. For Total FCE, the source equations and a one-sector counterexample identify a second coding problem beyond the disputed orientation of the consumption weights. The best-supported reconstruction gives **29.42%**, outside the project’s entire 15.0–23.2% range. The choice of allocation changes the conclusion about exceeding a climate threshold even before changing any manufacturing decision. ## Total GVA reproduces after correcting the published implementation

Let each index identify one producing industry in one region. Let $x_i$ be gross output, $V_i$ direct GVA, $A_{ij}$ the output of supplier $i$ needed per unit of buyer $j$’s output, and $L=(I-A)^{-1}$. Write $v_i=V_i/x_i$ for positive output. For target $j$, production triggered throughout the economy by a unit of final demand is column $j$ of $L$. Multiplying each entry by its own value-added intensity and summing gives embodied GVA per unit, $g_j=\sum_i v_iL_{ij}$. The type-I multiplier is $g_j/v_j$, and multiplying it by direct GVA yields **total GVA** $H_j=x_jg_j$. This last identity also avoids unnecessary division by direct GVA in positive-output sectors where it vanishes. [Hartmann et al., Eqs. 6–10](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07797-w)

With $Z_{ij}=A_{ij}x_j$, define first-tier origin shares $a_{rj}=(\sum_{i\in r}Z_{ij}+\mathbf1_{r=\mathrm{home}(j)}V_j)/(\sum_i Z_{ij}+V_j)$. Let $G_r$ be regional direct GVA and $p_r$ the region’s world population share. A fraction $a_{rj}H_j/G_r$ of that region's safe operating space (SOS) is attributed to the target, giving $F_j=H_j\sum_r p_r a_{rj}/G_r$. For network annual basic-price output $X$, the network share is $F_j X/x_j=Xg_j\sum_r p_ra_{rj}/G_r$. A larger or smaller sector by itself need not change this intensity. Its value-added technology and geographical origins do matter. Total GVA sectors overlap along supply chains. Their shares cannot be added to build a global budget or aggregated across nested supplier and customer activities. [Hartmann et al., Eqs. 1–4, 6–10 and usage notes](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07797-w)

For UK Chemicals n.e.c., all monetary totals in million current 2022 EUR, $x_j=44,744.269$, $V_j=16,233.039$, $g_j=0.9999999502$, $H_j=44,744.266$, and $a_{GB,j}=0.744516$. The factor is **0.0001972932187632**. In upstream NumPy, dividing a GVA array of shape `(n,)` by output of shape `(n,1)` produces an $n\times n$ matrix with entries $V_k/x_i$. Selecting column zero leaves the first sector’s GVA in every numerator. Replaying that operation yields $H_j=94,376.988$ and **0.000416141356598785**, matching the workbook to within $3\times10^{-17}$. The published factor is 2.109 times the source-equation estimate. This diagnosis is independently verified. A smaller issue is that upstream regional population weights sum to 1.00008893. Normalising them gives 0.0001972757. Retain the unnormalised result for publication replication and use a documented exact partition for a new application. [Upstream calculation, lines 695–737](https://github.com/RWTH-LTT/pbaesa/blob/d9329215521feba86005caac65bfa7d0404efb5a/pbaesa/allocation.py#L695-L737) · [Independent numerical record](<economic_output.txt>)

The aggregate also covers unrelated chemicals and biofuels. In the raw UK sector, the three compensation-of-employees entries and remaining net surplus are zero. Consumption of fixed capital is €16,225.50m and production taxes €7.54m, together accounting for all six-component direct GVA. SuperPro assigns 11.70% of production cost to labour. These are different accounting measures, but the absence of recorded sector wages is a concrete reason to qualify the use of sector-average supplier geography. Under a full-chain test, substituting $a_{rj}=\sum_{i\in r}v_iL_{ij}/g_j$ traces the location of embodied GVA directly. UK share becomes **68.41%**, sector factor **0.0002196246**, climate utilisation **58.30%**. First tier reproduces Hartmann. Full chain better answers where upstream value added arises. ## Ability-to-Pay changes whose economic circumstances count

Hjalsted’s rule for national entitlements is reproducible. For country $c$, let population be $P_c$, GDP $D_c$, per-capita income $y_c=D_c/P_c$, and world totals $P,D$. Allocate to each resident $B/P\times(D/P)/y_c\times\alpha$ of global SOS $B$. Summing over all people gives $\alpha=P^2/[D\sum_c(P_c^2/D_c)]$, and the **national share** $w_c=(P_c^2/D_c)/\sum_k(P_k^2/D_k)$. Compute this before aggregating countries to regions. Aggregating incomes first changes the inverse-income average. Hjalsted’s Eq. 6 proceeds to the local final-consumption share of a product or company serving consumers, and Eq. 7 includes a green-incentive variant. FYR instead sums the country weights into $w_r$ and substitutes $w_r$ for $p_r$ in Hartmann’s GVA equation. The new hybrid is interpretable if the ethical decision is to allocate by **producer-region capacity**. Neither company finances nor the needs of patients enter that formula. A Hjalsted-style product comparator would require countries of consumption and appropriate local expenditure shares. [Hjalsted et al., Table 2 and case studies](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/234548766/Hjalsted_2020_.pdf) · [Supplementary Eq. S1 and data sources](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1111%2Fjiec.13050/MediaObjects/44498_2021_2501001_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)

The 218-economy 2022 reconstruction gives 7,988,628,923 people, GDP of US$102.797 trillion and **α = 0.22107256**. For the UK, 67,636,000 people and US$47,034.78/head give a 0.84665% population share and **0.051207% of global ATP entitlement**. Regional and country weights sum to one. Dropping six remaining income-median imputations moves central utilisation from 153.459% to 153.484%. The FYR 2017 calculation gives 0.23408 against Hjalsted’s 0.243. The original used 2019-vintage World Data Bank GDP and UN Statistics Division population. The project uses World Bank population and IMF supplementation. Coverage, revisions and the population source all need to be considered before attributing that residual to data vintage. ## Total FCE counts the producer’s own final sales once

Hartmann’s Eq. 5 refers the downstream scaling to Oosterhoff. Hartmann’s worked discussion specifies that a sector selling only to final customers retains its direct FCE, while its upstream suppliers obtain a share of the final expenditure they enable. Read Oosterhoff’s Eqs. 3–4 together. The diagonal of its attribution matrix is assigned separately, and the adjusted distribution of intermediate contributions applies to off-diagonal entries. In Eq. 7, consumption weights belong to the **final product purchased**. The upstream adjustment in Eq. 4 belongs to the **supplying industry**. [Hartmann et al., pp. 3–4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07797-w) · [Oosterhoff et al., Eqs. 2–9 and Fig. 2](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/311056680/1_s2.0_S0959652623007321_main.pdf)

Let $d_{kr}$ be industry $k$’s share of household, nonprofit institutions serving households, and government final consumption in purchasing region $r$. Set $c_k=\sum_i L_{ik}$ and $f_i=(c_i-L_{ii})/L_{ii}$. The normalised contribution of final product $k$ to supplier $i$ is $K_{ik}=L_{ik}(1+f_i)/c_k$ off diagonal and $K_{ii}=1$. Since $L_{ii}(1+f_i)=c_i$, the off-diagonal expression already equals one on the diagonal. Thus $F_i=\sum_r p_r\sum_k K_{ik}d_{kr}$, with no extra direct-FCE term. Regional expenditure shares sum to one within numerical precision. The attribution grants different actors credit along an overlapping chain, so normalising each column of the final $K$ matrix to sum to one would implement a different rule. [Oosterhoff et al., pp. 7–9](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/311056680/1_s2.0_S0959652623007321_main.pdf) · [Numerical reconstruction](<economic.md>)

The upstream and both FYR variants return **two**. For the UK sector, the FR-only variant exceeds the source-equation result by **0.0001600291**, exactly the sector’s direct FCE factor. Conversely, a positive-output business-to-business supplier with zero direct final sales can enable downstream consumption. China’s electricity distribution and trade sector has zero published FCE but a reconstructed total factor of **0.00141890**. Upstream weighting by its own sales loses that activity. These are distinct from intentional overlap between suppliers and buyers. The 29.42% estimate uses Hartmann’s standard 2022 tables and branch price and geography. Oosterhoff’s earlier case endogenised capital and used customer information. The result here is a **source-equation reconstruction**, pending author confirmation. The final-market mix of general UK chemicals remains a weak proxy for the patients or payers served by this mAb. ## Product valuation and inventory coverage govern the threshold comparison

The SuperPro ICR verifies **US$92,098.02/kg in 2019 prices**, or **$92.09802/g**, for 562.97 kg/year from 121 modelled batches. The joint reference plan runs 320 + 320 + 221 batches, producing 4,003.65 kg/year at the rounded 4.65 kg/batch. The conversion to a 2022 UK sector-equivalent value is $4{,}003{,}650\times92.09802\div1.2766\times(90.6/81.1)\div0.85276=378{,}383{,}404$ EUR, or €378.3834m. The terms convert USD to 2019 GBP, apply the UK GDP deflator, then convert 2022 GBP to EUR. Dividing by €44,744.27m gives a share of **0.84566%**, applied once to the sector budget. This is an internally consistent zero-margin valuation scenario. The realised price receivable at the factory gate is unobserved. Cost can exceed price, and customer-owned inputs under tolling arrangements, internal transfers or differing capacity utilisation can materially change the comparison. SuperPro attributes **53.36%** of its cost to facilities. Extrapolating the unit cost unchanged from one 121-batch facility to three differently loaded sites is consequential. Estimate a comparable bulk drug-substance price or a cost-plus range with a stated ownership and utilisation basis. At the central exchange and inflation assumptions the break-even 2019 values for the partial climate footprint are **$59.77/g under GVA and $141.4/g under ATP**. ## Economic envelopes support scenarios and leave feasibility open

The reported **46.3–104.3% GVA** and **84.2–1,720% ATP** are reproducible economic scenario extrema conditional on fixed environmental coefficients and, for ATP, frozen 2022 market-rate income weights. They span 100% even before addressing excluded inventory. They have no probability interpretation. Crossing the project's three available material proxies with three electricity assumptions gives **55.46–65.27%** under the central GVA budget. Crossing just these physical cases independently with the published economic endpoints expands the intervals to **39.54–104.86% GVA and 71.93–1,729.63% ATP**. With the central inventory and the alternative preparations price index, upper endpoints reach **106.89% and 1,763.09%**. With a 2017 PPP weight case the ATP lower endpoint reaches 82.35% even before inventory variation. These illustrate specific omissions, without establishing ultimate bounds. For physical sourcing alternatives, vary supplier-specific environmental loads and transport with geography. A fixed footprint is appropriate when varying only the ethical assignment for an unchanged design. [Branch scenario generator](https://github.com/ALIFEPLAYS/FYR_code/blob/035538e33cd0223a5805ece2632e86b03e35eac4/2b_PB_Allocation/tools/finalise_allocation.py) · [Physical cross-checks](<boundary.md>) · [Income and geography sensitivity](<equity.md>)

**Before integration**, freeze the functional service demand, bulk-product gate, budget-year convention, producer valuation and allocation geography for each named scenario. Correct the FCE diagonal and weights, the incorrect regional concentration statement and the unsupported assertions about margins, inflation bounds and filling. Document the country crosswalk and retain separate published-replication and study-reference factors. For primary GVA, report price and inventory scenarios alongside the sector-average result. For ATP, state whose capacity is being represented and how country of production is inferred. Product-specific transfer values, procurement by manufacturing origin, and an inventory showing exclusions, their likely signs and plausible magnitudes are the additional data with the highest decision value. [Allocation methodology audited](https://github.com/ALIFEPLAYS/FYR_code/blob/035538e33cd0223a5805ece2632e86b03e35eac4/2b_PB_Allocation/METHODOLOGY.md)

For the exploratory MILP, impose a dimensionless fixed-budget row $\sum_k a_{bk}q_k/B_b\leq1$ for each selected indicator, with explicit units for each decision coefficient. Include fixed burdens on the left when applicable. Keep one-count attribution of electricity, treatment, reuse and avoided burdens, and control decisions with missing coefficients so an uncharacterised activity cannot win by default. Validate row evaluations against the frozen plan, recompute residuals in native units and compare the minimum achievable utilisation and feasible designs across allocation scenarios. If the solver can expand its entitlement merely by spending more, manufacturing surplus or reassigning nominal purchasing geography, the optimisation answers a different ethical question. At fixed ATP budget the reference plan needs a **34.8%** reduction in its *characterised subtotal* to meet the threshold. Establishing infeasibility requires minimising the load over admissible designs or solving the full constrained problem. [MILP model description](https://github.com/ALIFEPLAYS/FYR_code/blob/035538e33cd0223a5805ece2632e86b03e35eac4/4_Joint_MILP/with_LCA/MODEL_DETAILS.md)

The strongest defensible research claim is that the reference plan changes from below to above its modelled climate threshold when responsibility shifts from equal per-capita regional entitlements to income-weighted producer-region entitlements. This is already a meaningful result for chemical engineering design. An absolute sustainability claim requires evidence that the chosen economic value, geographical attribution and environmental inventory describe the same delivered product.
