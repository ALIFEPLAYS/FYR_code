# Product valuation and environmental boundary

### Takeaway

The $92.09802/g number and the sector classification are verifiable. The transfer from modelled plant cost to sector-equivalent output is an assumption which could err in either direction. The sector and national average margins are useful sensitivities, not bounds.

### Inferences

- Average full production cost is plausible as a zero-margin processing scenario. Loss-making production, site utilisation, different facility capital, transfer prices, contractual toll manufacturing with customer-owned materials and product-specific margins can move realised producer value in either direction. 53% facility cost makes linear extrapolation from one 121-batch facility to three differently loaded sites especially consequential. UK re-pricing assumes a 2019 US-dollar design can be localised through FX alone; check actual UK labour, energy, purchased materials and asset cost basis separately.
- The ratio to sector output presumes the marginal product has the sector's origin/multiplier intensity. Collapsing sector size algebraically leaves these sector intensities. It does not justify replacing sector output by the smaller pharma denominator without separately recalibrating the sector factor. Interpret as a hypothetical reference-economy share, not margin, direct GVA, availability, or actual national 2022 production.
- At fixed environmental subtotal, same conversion and origin, central GVA crosses 100% at $59.77/g and ATP at $141.33/g (2019 basis). Any price should refer to ex-works bulk antibody, not patient treatment or retail list price.

### Gaps

- No age-, scale-, site- and gate-matched transaction, arm's-length range or supplier accounts establish the price or its plausible bounds. A redesign may instead treat a calibrated reference production value as exogenous and state this ethical decision.

### Inferences

- Nine indicator ratios share one allocation scalar. Changing ethical allocation alone does not reorder the indicators for a fixed technology or change their relative targets. When comparing methodologies, avoid counting correlated outputs as nine corroborations.
- The partial LCIA represents one year's activity in the method's native state/flow units; no extra “per year” should be appended to radiative forcing, and it is not an independently renewable allocation to accumulate across years.

### Gaps

- No independent recomputation from all raw ecoinvent exchanges; real measured production, geographic supply, completeness and TPE identity remain unverified. A hash authenticates a particular file, not the inventory in it.

## Are the sensitivities and constraints ready?

### Takeaway

Use a fixed reference demand and fixed external budget, and keep the equity case visibly conditional. A scenario-level cap or feasibility experiment can be scientifically useful. Do not infer network sustainability or infeasibility from screening outcomes.

### Cited Findings

- Independently crossed three saved materials (PU foam, emulsion SBR and solution SBR proxies) and three electricity replication bases (batch, site and line), using 3 sites / 8 assigned lines. For identical GVA budget and sector sourcing this gives 55.46–65.27% climate (central 64.899%). For example emulsion SBR/site yields 9.25326e−7 W/m²; solution SBR/batch yields 1.08899e−6. Main envelope code crosses value basis and supplier origins only, holding impacts and ATP weights fixed (GDP PPP is separately tabulated). Crossing just these existing 3×3 numerators with the archived extrema yields **39.54–104.86% GVA, 71.93–1,729.63% ATP**. These are demonstrative bounds over those discrete cases only; using the alternative C212 price index changes upper bounds further. [Scenario generator](https://github.com/ALIFEPLAYS/FYR_code/blob/035538e33cd0223a5805ece2632e86b03e35eac4/2b_PB_Allocation/tools/finalise_allocation.py) (local audit source: Physical coefficients)
- Current joint model fixes total batches and selected sites/lines, allows production to move among sites, has product freight cost without distribution/cold-storage climate factors, and includes industrial-symbiosis carbon credits in a separate objective. [Design details](https://github.com/ALIFEPLAYS/FYR_code/blob/035538e33cd0223a5805ece2632e86b03e35eac4/4_Joint_MILP/with_LCA/MODEL_DETAILS.md)

### Inferences

- Choose one budget and numerator definition externally; do not let the solver increase prices, manufacture extra units or move orders to low-income suppliers to enlarge the entitlement. Use dimensionless normalised constraint sum(a_kj x_j)/B_k ≤ 1 (or fixed exogenous epsilon) to avoid solver feasibility tolerance on 1e−6-scale quantities. Verify residuals in native units and bind exactly once each electricity/resin/wastewater/biomass/substitution term. Test inactive categories, route-wise marginal coefficients and omitted impacts before releasing new decision options; an unrepresented option must not win by earning zero burden. Conduct a fixed-demand minimum-achievable utilisation solve and compare feasible sets and decisions. A saved solution above 100% is not proof that no feasible network exists. Keep credits separate in the main attributed accounting; consider sensitivity to complete physical quantities and full supply-chain data. Conditional exploratory constraints can be issued before resolving every gap if decision domains and inference are explicitly restricted.

### Gaps

- Actual operating point, underlying raw sourcing, transfer prices and confidence limits remain unknown. No numeric dominance of any category or safety claim survives all possible inventories merely because it survives the small set tested.
