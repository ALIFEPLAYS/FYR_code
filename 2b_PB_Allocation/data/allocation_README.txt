Allocation Factors for Absolute Environmental Sustainability Assessment

This dataset includes allocation factors for 163 economic sectors across 44 countries and 5 Rest-of-World regions to determine environmental sustainability thresholds.
The allocation factors are calculated using four economic allocation methods, based on direct or total, and on consumption- or production-based value generated by each sector.
